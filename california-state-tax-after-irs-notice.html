California Taxpayers: Your IRS Notice and the Franchise Tax Board

Last updated 2026-11-02 · State tax follow-ups
Short answer

Your IRS notice is handled under federal rules, and the California Franchise Tax Board is a separate agency that may write to you on its own. Answer the IRS by the date on your notice, and read any California letter and the agency's official site separately.

Start with the letter in your hand

Say you are a freelance designer and a CP2000 arrives saying the income reported by your clients does not match your return. That letter comes from the IRS, and the federal guides on this site apply to it. The IRS describes a CP2000 as a proposed change, not a bill, and it asks for a reply by the date printed on the notice.

Before you think about California at all, find that date, read what the IRS says it changed, and decide whether you agree. Our guide to the CP2000 notice walks through those steps.

Who the Franchise Tax Board is

California's tax agency is the Franchise Tax Board (ftb.ca.gov). It is a different agency from the IRS, with its own letters, its own contact channels and its own instructions.

Whether the Franchise Tax Board contacts you, when, and about what are questions only that agency can answer. This page does not try to predict it, and it does not describe California rules.

Two agencies, two sets of instructions

Treat each letter on its own terms. A reply to the IRS does not automatically answer a California letter, and the reverse is also true.

  • Keep federal and California letters in separate folders.
  • Note the sender, the notice number and the response date on each one.
  • Answer each agency using the instructions it gave you.

Checking that a California letter is real

The IRS says it usually makes first contact by mail and that an unexpected call, text or social media message demanding payment or personal details is a warning sign. It is sensible to apply the same caution to anything claiming to be from California's tax agency.

Rather than using a phone number or link from the message, go to ftb.ca.gov yourself and look for the agency's own contact information.

Frequently asked questions

Will the Franchise Tax Board contact me because of my IRS notice?

This site cannot say. The Franchise Tax Board is a separate agency, and only it can tell you whether it has a question for you. If a letter arrives, read it and check ftb.ca.gov.

Does answering the IRS also answer California?

No. Each agency sets its own instructions and response dates. Reply to each one separately, following what its letter says.

Where do I find the California agency's official information?

The Franchise Tax Board's site is ftb.ca.gov. Type the address into your browser instead of following a link in a message you did not expect.

Can the tool write my California response?

The tool helps you organize facts and draft a response letter for your federal matter. It does not replace the state agency's own instructions.
